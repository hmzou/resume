✍️ Writing Strong Experience (Two Methods)
🔹 Prompt 4 — Experience Rewriting (Structured R–M–R)

R–M–R = Responsibility – Methods – Results

Rewrite my experience bullet points using this structure:
1. Responsibility
2. Methods / tools / skills used
3. Results or outcomes

Do not add false information.
Quantify results where possible.
Keep it ATS-friendly and professional.


Best for:

Students with limited experience

Roles without clear metrics

Improving clarity and structure

🔹 Prompt 5 — Experience Rewriting (Google XYZ Formula)

Accomplished X, by doing Y, as measured by Z

Rewrite my experience bullet points using the Google XYZ formula:

- Accomplished X
- By doing Y
- As measured by Z

Do not invent numbers.
If exact metrics are unavailable, suggest realistic qualitative outcomes.
Keep it ATS-friendly.


Best for:

Tech, business, data, and analytics roles

Experience with measurable impact

Stronger, results-focused bullets

🧠 Important Notes

AI should never invent experience or numbers

Quantification can include:

Time saved

Number of people/projects

Frequency (weekly, monthly)

Skills gained or responsibility level

You stay responsible for the final content

You are the pilot.
AI is the copilot.
